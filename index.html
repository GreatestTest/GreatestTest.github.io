Frequently asked questions

Do you guarantee first-page rankings?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee a position in search results. We agree measurable goals, test hypotheses and report the numbers.

Who do you work with?

Small businesses, mid-sized companies and large companies that need a contractor for a specific direction. Small projects are welcome.

Can I order just one thing, such as an audit or a bot?

Yes. You can take a single task or hand us the whole direction.

How is the price set?

Prices are individual. We show a starting price for each service and confirm the final price after the brief and an agreement on the scope.

Which websites do you build?

Landing pages, company sites on WordPress and online stores on OpenCart. We also improve existing sites.

How do we communicate?

After you send a request, we reply by email or Telegram, whichever you choose, and offer a call if it helps.

Which languages do you work in?

The site and our communication are available in English and Russian.
