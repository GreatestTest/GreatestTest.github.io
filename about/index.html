A digital agency that works like an engineering team

GDT is built on one idea: marketing decisions should be tested and measured, and the technical side should simply work.

Who is behind GDT

GDT (Greatest Digital Technology) grew out of freelance practice. The founder talked to clients directly, set the tasks, built the solutions and delivered the results. Along the way came experience with WordPress and OpenCart websites, Telegram bots connected to a CRM, automated reporting, and Python scripts for parsing and data collection, together with a background in QA and analytics.

That background explains our habits: we check, we test, and we look at numbers rather than at how something looks. Today GDT works as a team, and the focus is SEO, Google Ads and Meta Ads.

How we think

Our approach is practical: hypothesis, test, numbers, scale. We do not promise first-page rankings in a week. We show what happens with your budget and your requests, using real data.

What this gives you

  • You talk to the people who do the work, not to a sales manager.
  • One team understands both marketing and the technical side: bots, websites and integrations.
  • One contractor covers several tasks, so you do not need to hire three.

Who we work with

  • Small businesses that need a predictable flow of requests without an in-house marketing team.
  • Mid-sized companies that have a product but no system for attracting customers.
  • Large companies that need a contractor for a specific direction or project.
