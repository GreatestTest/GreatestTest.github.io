SEO: technical optimisation and growth in search

Technical fixes, structure and content that help your site be found by the people who search for what you sell.

Who it is for

Companies with a website that want more targeted visitors from search, including sites in more than one language.

The problem it solves

The site exists but is hard to find: pages are not indexed, structure is unclear, speed is poor, or content does not match what people search for.

Price

What is included

  • Technical audit and fixes
  • Semantic core and search intent
  • On-page optimisation: titles, headings, structure and content
  • Internal linking and site architecture
  • Structured data
  • Multilingual SEO: language versions and hreflang
  • Regular reports from Search Console and analytics

How it works

  1. Audit

    We find what holds the site back in search.

  2. Plan

    We rank tasks by expected effect and agree the order.

  3. Implementation

    We fix the technical side and improve pages and content.

  4. Measurement

    We follow indexation, queries and traffic and report the numbers.

Questions and answers

Can you guarantee a top position?

No. Nobody can honestly guarantee a position in search results. We agree measurable goals and report what happens.

How soon will results appear?

It depends on the site and the competition. We discuss realistic expectations after the audit.

Do you work with multilingual sites?

Yes, including language versions, hreflang and separate keyword research for each language.
