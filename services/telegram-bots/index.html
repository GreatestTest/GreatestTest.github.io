Telegram bots for leads, funnels and CRM

A bot that collects requests, qualifies them and passes them to your CRM or a spreadsheet, so no lead is lost.

Discuss your task

Who it is for

Businesses that receive requests in chats and lose some of them, or that want to automate first contact with customers.

The problem it solves

Requests arrive from several places, managers reply late, and nobody knows how many leads were lost on the way.

Price

Price on request

Final price is individual and confirmed after all details are agreed.

All prices

What is included

  • Lead capture: forms, questions and qualification inside Telegram
  • Funnels and automated replies
  • Integration with your CRM and Google Sheets
  • Notifications for your team
  • Admin tools and basic statistics

How it works

  1. Task analysis

    We learn how requests reach you now and where they get lost.

  2. Scenario

    We design the dialogue and the data the bot collects.

  3. Build and integration

    We build the bot and connect it to your CRM or spreadsheet.

  4. Testing and launch

    We test every branch of the dialogue before real customers see it.

  5. Support

    We fix issues and adjust the scenario based on real conversations.

Questions and answers

Which CRM can the bot connect to?

Most systems that have an API, and Google Sheets. We confirm the integration after reviewing your CRM.

Who hosts the bot?

Hosting options are agreed per project.

Can the bot be changed later?

Yes. Scenarios, questions and integrations can be adjusted as your process changes.
